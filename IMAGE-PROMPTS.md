# Image prompts for ChatGPT — Shreya & Jatin wedding website

How to use this file
1. Open one new ChatGPT chat and paste the **style guide** first. Keep every image in that same chat so they all match.
2. Then paste one prompt at a time. Download each result at full size and rename it to the **file name** shown.
3. If ChatGPT gives a white or checkered background where it says *transparent*, reply: "Please regenerate as a PNG with a truly transparent background."
4. Send the files back here. Claude will place them, optimise them for mobile, and keep the line-art versions as a fallback.

You don't need all of them. The ones marked ★ make the biggest difference.

## Style guide (paste this first)

> I'm designing a luxury Indian (Rajasthani / Marwari) wedding invitation website for Shreya & Jatin. Every image you make in this chat must share one consistent style:
> - Fine hand-painted watercolour illustration with delicate gold-foil line detailing, like a premium printed wedding card from Jaipur.
> - Palette only: deep maroon #5A1022, wine #7A2236, antique gold #B08D57, champagne #F2E5CB, warm ivory #FBF6EE, with marigold orange and haldi yellow as accents. No neon, no pure black, no blue.
> - Elegant, airy, editorial composition with generous empty space. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks, no borders unless asked.
> - People, when shown, are illustrated (not photographs). Faces are soft and minimal or seen from behind, in traditional Rajasthani wedding attire. They should not look like any specific real person.
> - High resolution, crisp edges, no blur, no AI artefacts on hands.
> Reply "ready" and wait for my image requests.
